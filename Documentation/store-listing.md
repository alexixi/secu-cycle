### First comment (maker's comment)

```
Hi there 👋

I'm one of the makers of Sécu'Cycle.

Every bike navigation app we tried optimized for the same thing: getting there fast. But the number-one reason people don't ride is not distance — it's the fear of ending up in traffic. So we built the app we wanted: one that optimizes for how safe a route feels, and only then for how long it takes.

How it works:
• You enter a start and a destination, and we return several routes with an explicit safety/time trade-off — you pick the one you're comfortable with.
• Turn-by-turn navigation with voice instructions, so you can keep the phone in your pocket.
• Routes adapt to your profile: bike type (e-bikes included), fitness level, personal preferences.
• Riders report hazards — accidents, roadworks, obstacles — and those reports feed straight back into the routing for everyone.

It's totally free :)

We'd love your feedback, especially on the safety scoring: what makes a street feel safe to *you*? Happy to answer anything in the comments.
```
